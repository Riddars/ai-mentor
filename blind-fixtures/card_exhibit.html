Lantern Survey: painted card exhibit

A fictional display from the imagined town of Velin.

Collection note

Student observers arranged cardboard stations around six invented zones. Each card records a painted marker, a tile count, and a paper vane reading.

Reading guide

The light dial runs from zero to nine. The breeze dial runs from zero to four. The exhibit uses imaginary data and carries no real participant information.
