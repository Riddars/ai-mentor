# Lantern Survey: calibration journal

This journal belongs to a wholly fictional student project in the town of Velin.
The entries describe paper instruments and invented readings.

## Materials

- Twelve cardboard stations labeled V01-V12
- Painted paper vane, graphite ruler, and ceramic counting tiles
- A nine-step light dial and a five-step breeze dial

## Week 01: rehearsal

**Question.** Does the courtyard station keep the same marker after three rotations?

1. Place tile cobalt beside station V02.
2. Read the vane before moving the light dial.
3. Record the dial twice, with the second reading delayed by one bell interval.
4. Store the card in a sleeve bearing the week number.

**Observation.** The imagined tile count was 8; the vane crossed 3 grid squares.
**Decision.** Keep the south edge as the reference for the next session.
